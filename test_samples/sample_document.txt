QuickConvert: Modern Windows 11 Document Converter
==================================================

Features:
- Radial glassmorphism user interface
- High fidelity PDF to image & text conversions
- Unicode text support: Привет мир, こんにちは, café, naïve
- Strict overwrite safety and clean architecture

Paragraph 2:
QuickConvert automatically derives non-colliding output filenames
and handles multipage exports with sequential numbering.
